Lore

Harrison Shepherd, the owner of a beautiful hotel right outside the small town of *****************, has the power to create evil creatures that possess plush toys of hybrid animals. He places these plushes on the beds within the hotel rooms, and when guests go to sleep near these plushes, their soul and the soul of the evil creature swap. The humans (now called Dreamers) find themselves in a hotel lobby eerily similar to the lobby where they just checked in. They can't remember much about their lives before falling asleep, and it seems like most of their memories have been replaced. At the reception desk is a hybrid sheep-spider boy named Hauntli, who tells the Dreamers that he needs them to brave the floors of the hotel to extract Fear Dust from the Crystalized Fear deposites that appear within the rooms. However, he warns the Dreamers that past Dreamers have been corrupted by the Fear Dust and became Nightmares: aggresive creatures that attack anything that moves on sight. Hauntli tells the Dreamers that extracting this Fear Dust will help stop the corruption and clear a path to the top floor, wherein the Dreamers can escape the hotel and return to the real world.


In reality, Hauntli is using the extracted Fear Dust to create more Nightmares, intending to keep the Dreamers in his realm of sleep forever.


Originally, Harrison was the director of a TV show for young audiences. His son, Sammy, loved the show, and as such, loved the characters. Due to a terminal illness, Sammy ends up passing away in his early teens, and Harrison falls into a deep depression. He retires and sells the rights to the TV show. As the days pass, his mental state deteriorates, and eventually, his desperation to see his son again leads him to discover dark magic (how is TBD). This dark magic allows him to summon evil creatures from another realm, but they need a vessel to support them. Harrison learns that the plushes of the characters from his show are adequate temporary vessels, but the creatures tell him that human bodies would make perfect permanent vessels. In exchange for giving them permanent vessels, the creatures promise to send the humans' souls they replace to Sammy, who can then spend his afterlife with them as his friends. He listens to the creatures, and they explain that they can easily possess humans if they are asleep, so one night, he sets the plush of the character Serenity from the TV show on his wife's nightstand. He wanted the first person to go see his son again to be the boy's mother. The next morning, the swap of souls had worked, and after that, Harrison opens the hotel with the intent to send more friends to Sammy. He slowly spirals into insanity with the need to get more friends to his son so he'd never be alone, inevitably sending hundreds of people to this alternate realm of dreams and nightmares.
