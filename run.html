In A Run

  • A Dreamer enters the elevator to create a run, where they can choose the max number of players and whether or not the run is Friends Only. [maybe other settings?]
    • Once the party is full, the elevator shuts and a door on the other wall opens, revealing Silver's Pop-Up Kitchen. Dreamers are able to buy any dishes they've unlocked by buying recipes from Silver in the lobby. Everyone starts with access to one dish that costs 2 Moon Tokens, which increases the amount of Moon Tokens gained by +1.
  • Once in the run, Dreamers are brought to different floors of the hotel, where deposits of Crystalized Fear have formed in cracks that appear in the floors and walls. Nightmares stalk the halls, chasing and attacking any Dreamer that comes into their line of sight. Items are scattered across the floor that Dreamers are able to pick up, along with run-only currency called Stars, which appear as small piles of glow-in-the-dark stars on the floor. One cluster gives +5 Stars.
    • Drills have been placed atop the deposites, and the Dreamers are tasked to operate the drills to extract the Fear Dust. Extracted Fear Dust fills a large tank in the middle of the floor that appears to connect to the upper floors. Once the tank is full (all desposites have been extracted), the Dreamers must rush back to the elevator before it closes and leaves without them.
    • Dreamers gain +1 Moon Token per completed operation, unless increased by a Silver dish or card boost. Only Dreamers who contributed at least 20% to the machine's completion get the Token(s).
  • Inside the elevator after escaping the floor, the door that opened up to Silver's Kitchen now opens up to Hauntli's Shop, where he sells items for Stars that can aid Dreamers during their run. One of the other walls has a TV screen that shows two "cards" which the Dreamers can vote on which they want. These cards provide run-only modifiers and have a range of effects. There is a chance for a Hauntli Card to spawn instead of the two cards, which allows Dreamers to vote whether or not they wish to attempt Hauntli's floor.
    • The first time Dreamers successfully beat Hauntli's Floor in a run, they are each rewarded with a 20% Sanity Drain reduction modifier. Every subsequent completed Hauntli Floor only gives a 5% Sanity Drain reduction. Dreamers must complete at least 5 floors after beating Hauntli before his card has the chance to spawn again.
