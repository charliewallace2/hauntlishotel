Dreamers

Jump to List of Dreamers

**This page is still under development**

Dreamers are the playable characters in Hauntli's Hotel. You earn Moon Tokens during runs in order to unlock new Dreamers with Cali in the Lobby. Each Dreamer has unique stat spreads and abilities that adhear to different playstyles; Operators, Supporters, and Healers.

  • There are two kinds of Supports: Distraction Supports and Stats Supports.
    • Distraction Supports typically have low Stealth and high Speed and Sanity, allowing them to take Nightmares' attention off other Dreamers in sticky situations. However, this usually means that these Supports have low Operation and Health stats, and having Nightmares on them too long will cause them to have issues with retaining Stamina due to low Sanity.
    • Stats Supports have abilities that aid their fellow Dreamers in some way, either by regenerating Sanity or Stamina or providing Speed, Stealth, or Operation boosts. These Supports usually have high Stamina and Health, making it easy to stay alive longer in order to help their teammates.
  • Some Dreamers may be considered "All-Around", where they have stat spreads that are relatively equal.
  • Mythical Dreamers have one stat maxed out and one extra notch in another stat, totalling 19 notches. These Dreamers also have abilities that are extremely useful, making them heavily-sought Dreamers, but also making them expensive and hard to obtain. Mythical Dreamers are also the only Dreamers to be hybrids that include a mythical creature.
  • New players have the option to choose between two starter Dreamers, which are both considered All-Around Dreamers.
  • Dreamer prices depend on the Lethality of their corrosponding Nightmare; the more rare the Nightmare, the more expensive the Dreamer.

Stat List

(Max 5 per stat, 18 total, Mythicals get 19)

  • Health (Red)
    • The base number of health a Dreamer has.
  • Sanity (Orange)
    • Total Sanity amount and how quickly the Dreamer recovers Sanity. Dreamer Sanity drops while being seen by a Nightmare and recovers passively when not being seen. Dreamers recover Sanity quicker around other Dreamers, and even faster when in the light. Low Sanity decreases Operation, also making it difficult for players to see by darkening and blurring their screen. If a Dreamer reaches zero Sanity, their Stamina Drain is increased by 25%. Once Sanity is above zero, the modifier is removed. Dreamers cannot passively regenerate Sanity while being chased by a Nightmare, regardless of modifiers (unless provided instant Sanity by a Stats Supporter or item).
  • Speed (Yellow)
    • Movement speed of the Dreamer. Increased while sprinting.
  • Stamina (Green)
    • The total duration a Dreamer can sprint for.
  • Operation (Blue)
    • How quickly a Dreamer completes machines.
  • Stealth (Purple)
    • How easily a Dreamer is seen by a Nightmare.

Cali

[stat sheet]

[images]

Nightmare

Cali is a stationary Nightmare who can spawn anywhere in The Hallways floor. She has a radius around her that will alert her if a player sprints inside it, causing her to wake up. Once awake, her heads will snake out from her body and each attack any player within the radius for 10 damage. Each head gives a different debuff; Happy gives a Speed debuff, Sad gives an Operation debuff, and Angry gives a Sanity Regen debuff. If no players are in her radius for 10 seconds, she will go back to sleep.

Cobalt

[stat sheet]

[images]

Nightmare

Cobalt is a slow Nightmare that stalks around the map, leaving a trail of Fear-colored fire in their wake. If a player comes into contact with the flames, they take 2 damage every second they stay in it. Additionally, whenever someone fails a skillcheck, Cobalt rushes to that machine and sets it on fire, making the machine temporarily unusable and removing 10% from its completion.

Nittary

[stat sheet]

[images]

Nightmare

On Nittary's map, there’s 1 door that’s open that leads to Nittary's room, where you can find Nittary's nest. He is resting in the nest, while a little wind up rabbit toy follows the person who’s closest to it (it roams around until it sees someone). If the wind up rabbit comes in contact with a player, Nittary will be alerted and wake up, rushing twards the wind up rabbit as it maks a screeching noise. Once nittary is out of his nest, he will be out for the rest of the floor, but as long as you don't move while in his line of sight, he won’t chase you. The wind-up rabbit will not alert if it comes in contact with a player on a machine.

North

[stat sheet]

[images]

Nightmare

North is the fastest Nightmare, having a Speed stat that makes her faster than Dreamers with a 5 in Speed. However, she's also the youngest Nightmare, and as such she tires easily; after chasing for an accumulative 60 seconds, she immediately stops chasing and lies down to take a nap. Her nap lasts 30 seconds. She also has the ability to bounce over obstacles using her tail, which has a cooldown of 25 seconds.

Serenity

[stat sheet]

[images]

Nightmare

Serenity has two phases she switches between, her Staring phase and her Weeping phase. Her Staring phase only lasts 5 seconds, while her Weeping phase lasts 30 seconds, with a 2 second buffer in between each phase. In her Weeping phase, she targets the player closest to her and chases them if she isn't in their line of sight. In her Staring phase, anyone who's looking at her will gain the Petrified modifier, freezing them in place until she returns to her Weeping phase.

Silver

[stat sheet]

[images]

Nightmare

Silver is very fast, easily being able to keep up with Dreamers with a 4 or 5 Speed stat. His main attack is his fire breath, which does 20 burn damage and has a cool down of 45 seconds. During his cooldown, he will use his claws in a swiping attack, each swipe doing 5 damage.
