Hauntli's Hotel is a passion project game that is currently in the early development stage, and will be for the forseeable future. It follows (and is heavily based off of) the same structure as games like Dandy's World, Dolly's Factory, and Toons Universe on ROBLOX, where players brave various floors and enemies to complete tasks in order to gain in-game currency to buy playable characters and other in-game content. The navigation menu above will take you to pages with more information about specific aspects of the game.
