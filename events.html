Floor Events

Occationally, a floor can spawn with a Floor Event, which can help or hinder players in their run.

  • Dim Floors (common)
    • The lights dim, dropping Sanity Recovery in light and pausing Sanity Recovery in the dark, but increasing Sanity Recovery near other Dreamers. Dreamers naturally produce some light, unless otherwise stated.
  • Condensed Fear Floors (common)
    • A concetrated form of Fear Dust has created a gas that covers the floor. Dreamers have reduced Sanity and increased Speed.
  • Flooded Floors (rare)
    • The plumbing on this floor has corroded and caused a flood. Nightmare and Dreamer Speed is reduced. Dreamers also have slightly reduced Operation.
  • The Golden Ram (very rare)
    • On these floors, you can find a golden-colored version of Dreamer Florence. Upon talking to him, he will give you a small ball of Gold Fleece if you have room in your inventory. Using Gold Fleece will grant you a +50% to a stat of your choosing.
