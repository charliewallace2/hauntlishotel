Nightmares

Nightmares are the enemies you can encounter. They are past Dreamers that have been corrupted by the Crystalized Fear, which can be seen growing out of their bodies. They are aggressive and animalistic, making them impossible to be reasoned with.

  • As Dreamers progress further up the hotel, more and more Nightmares appear, and higher Lethality Nightmares show up more often. The more difficult a Nightmare is to deal with, the higher it's Lethality rating. Early-run Nightmares have a ★ to ★★ Lethality rating, Nightmare Mythicals have a ★★★★ rating, and Hauntli has a ★★★★★ rating.
  • Mythical Nightmares only spawn on their respective Mythical Floor, which is themed around their occupation. Mythical Nightmares are typically faster and do much more damage than regular Nightmares. Mythical Floors spawn every 5 floors, where two Mythicals can be found. Floors 5, 10, and 15 are guaranteed to have separate Mythical floors. Afterwards, it's completely random which Mythical floor will spawn.
    • The Kitchen – Silver and Cobalt
    • The Hallways – Nittary and Cali
    • The Storage Room – Serenity and North
  • Nightmares lose intrest in their target once they can no longer see them. However, some Nightmares are stationary and only attack when Dreamers get too close, and others may have a longer attention span and can chase Dreamers even after loosing sight of them. Being wary and treading with caution around Nightmares is encouraged.
  • When a Dreamer is seen by a Nightmare, they aquire 5% completion towards that Nightmare's Research. Encountering a Nightmare is the only way to earn their Research. Upon reaching 100% Research, the player gets 10-35 Moon Tokens (dependant on rarity).
