Lobby Shops

Inside the hotel lobby, on either side of the hall,there are three shops, each run by a different Mythical Dreamer. These Dreamers are called Staff, and they were the first six Dreamers to enter the hotel. They spend their days in the hotel aiding new Dreamers instead of trying to make it to the top. Their shops provide in-game items, boosts, and information.

  • Dreamer Shop
    • The Dreamer Shop is a small elevator off to the side of the main entrance of the hotel. Here you can find Cali, the Bellhop who can help you buy new Dreamers to play.
  • Relationships
    • This "shop" is a juice bar run by Cobalt. Here you can learn more about Dreamers' relationships with eachother, and check your current friendship levels with Staff. When you select two completed Pendants on the given screen, Cobalt will give you a piece of information about the two Dreamers the Pendants belong to.
  • Stickers
    • This shop, a little kiosk run by Nittary, sells stickers for Moon Tokens that allow players to communicate within runs without needing to use the chat. Nittary also sells some fun/silly stickers which are slightly more expensive.
  • Skins
    • North, the resident janitor and youngest Staff, runs her shop out of her painting studio. Here you can buy new skins for your aquired Dreamers with Moon Tokens. These skins will always be available; no limited-time or event skins will be sold by North.
  • Premium Skins & Gamepasses
    • Near the entrance of the hotel is a caravan covered in gold and displaying beautiful fabrics and jewlery. This is Serenity's shop, where you can buy premium and limited-time skins for Robux or Moon Tokens, though these skins are significantly more expensive than North's. You can also buy Gamepasses from Serenity (list of Gamepasses found in "Future Game Mechanics").
  • Recipies
    • In the lobby, Silver has set up a makeshift kitchen as an extension to the hotel's main kitchen. He uses this area to sell players new recipies that unlock dishes for future runs. The recipies are relatively expensive, but the price of the dish itself in Silver's pre-run Pop-Up Kitchen is much cheaper.
