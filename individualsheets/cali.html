About

Cali is a wild and fun punk rocker. The only thing that holds them back is their seperate heads. They can never agree on anything! While one wants to explore dangerous lands, another prefers drawing at home. The center head spends most of her time trying to help the other two agree on something. Despite this, they try to live life to the fullest. Nothing can stand in their way, not even their other heads.
