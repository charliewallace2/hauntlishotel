About

Cobalt is an adult tiger-phoenix hybrid with a calm demeanor and inviting atmosphere. They don't talk much, but people quickly feel comfortable around them and often find themselves in deep conversations with them. They care fiercely for people they consider close to them, and are more than willing to take the fall for a mistake should the situation call for it. They are the kind of person to silently do favors that makes life easier for others, like paying for food or being a safe space for those who need it.
